Top Five Latino Foods

Foods being rated and ranked

  • Empanadas

  • Tamales

Number five: Arepas

Arepas are a traditional dish from Venezuela and Colombia, made from ground maize dough or cooked flour. They can be grilled, baked, fried, boiled, or steamed, and are often stuffed with various fillings such as cheese, meats, or avocado.

Number four: Tamales

Tamales are a traditional Mesoamerican dish made of masa (a starchy dough, usually corn-based) which is steamed in a corn husk or banana leaf. The wrapping is discarded before eating. Tamales can be filled with meats, cheeses, fruits, vegetables, chilies, or any preparation according to taste.

Number three: Ceviche

Ceviche is a seafood dish popular in Latin American coastal regions, typically made from fresh raw fish cured in freshly squeezed key lime or bitter orange juice, with sliced onions, chili peppers, and seasoning. It is often accompanied by side dishes such as sweet potato, lettuce, corn, or avocado.

Number two: Empanadas

Empanadas are a type of pastry filled with a variety of ingredients such as meat, cheese, vegetables, or fruits. They are popular in many Latin American countries and can be baked or fried. The dough is typically made from wheat flour, and the filling varies by region and personal preference.

Number one: Tacos

Tacos are a traditional Mexican dish consisting of small hand-sized corn or wheat tortillas topped with a filling. The filling can include a variety of ingredients such as beef, pork, chicken, seafood, beans, vegetables, and cheese. Tacos are often garnished with salsa, guacamole, cilantro, tomatoes, onions, and lettuce.
