Chinese Famous Dishes

Chinese food is one of the oldest and most popular food styles in the world. It brings together thousands of years of history, unique regional flavors, and a deep focus on balancing tastes, colors, and textures. 10 Chinese Foods that are most popular: Peking Duck , kung pao chicken , sweet and sour pork , mapo tofu , dan dan noodles , lo mein , chow mein , egg drop soup , wanton soup and fried rice.

Fried Rice Ingredients • 3 cups cooked and chilled long-grain rice (preferably day-old) • 2 to 3 large eggs, beaten • 1 cup frozen peas and carrots • ½ cup diced white or yellow onion • 2 cloves garlic, minced • 2 to 3 tablespoons cooking oil (divided) • 2 tablespoons soy sauce • 1 teaspoon sesame oil • Chopped green onions for garnish

2. Dumplings Ingredients Mix the meat, cabbage, green onions, soy sauce, sesame oil, garlic, and ginger in a bowl. Put 1 tablespoon of filling in the center of each wrapper. Wet the edges with water and fold the wrapper in half. Press the edges together. Heat a pan with a little oil over medium heat. Place the dumplings in the pan and cook until the bottoms are golden. Add about ¼ cup of water and cover the pan. Steam for about 6–8 minutes, until the filling is fully cooked. Serve with soy sauce or your favorite dipping sauce.

3. Chow Mein Ingredients 8 oz chow mein noodles 1 lb chicken breast, sliced 1 cup cabbage, chopped 1 carrot, sliced ½ cup broccoli 2 green onions, chopped 2 tablespoons soy sauce 1 tablespoon oyster sauce 1 teaspoon sesame oil 1 tablespoon cooking oil 1 teaspoon garlic

4. Kung Pao Chicken Ingredients 1 lb chicken breast, cut into bite-sized pieces 1 cup cashews 2 tablespoons cooking oil 2 tablespoons soy sauce 1 tablespoon oyster sauce 1 teaspoon sesame oil 1 teaspoon garlic, minced 1 teaspoon ginger, minced 2 red chilies, sliced 2 green onions, chopped
